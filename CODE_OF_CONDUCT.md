# Code of Conduct

Tidy is a friendly place to learn, build and share. We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

**Be kind and constructive.** Assume good intent, explain your reasoning, and critique code and ideas, never people.
**Welcome newcomers.** Every expert was a beginner; answer questions patiently and label good first issues.
**Respect privacy and safety.** Don't post other people's personal files or paths in issues; redact them.
**No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks** of any kind.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that don't fit this code, and may ban repeat offenders. Report concerns by opening a confidential security/advisory report or contacting a maintainer through the repository's contact options.
